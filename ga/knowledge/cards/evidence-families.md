method: acceptance needs two independent evidence families
---
Evidence counts by family, not by count: two readings of one sheet by two models are one family; a reading and an
address-register lookup are two. A placement is accepted only when a second family agrees with the matcher that made
it (structure overlay, address point inside, another edition, a second matcher). Self-consistency (residual, score,
inlier ratio) proves consistency, not correctness. Every rule in the policy carries its measured accepted-wrong rate
on a frozen benchmark, and a change that raises missed errors is refused by the gate.
