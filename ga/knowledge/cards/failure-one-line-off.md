Measured on six survey sheets: two placements with residuals of 40 m at every node were 9 km off, exactly one
graticule interval, because the frame reader had taken the first interior graticule line for the neatline and the
corner values were assigned to the wrong line. Nothing inside the fit can see it: the lattice is self-consistent.
Guards, in order of cost: count the lines detected between the corners against the count the interval implies
(an off-by-one leaves one line unexplained at one edge); check the margin outside the chosen neatline for ticks or
figures that belong to the map (the collar is blank, the map face is not); compare with the sheet-index bounds
when the series is known; and always verify with a family that did not read the frame, a structure overlay against
water or rail, the neighbour sheet, another edition. The same fault appears with kilometre grids (one square off,
1 km) and with abbreviated grid figures completed wrongly (10 km or 100 km off). The two-family policy caught both
cases and sent them to review; the residual-only policy accepted them.
