Abstain is an answer and it is typed. no_location_cue: the reading found nothing that ties the sheet to the Earth
(floor plan, section, diagram). no_candidate_area: cues exist but no reference resolves them to a region (names in
no gazetteer, a series not indexed). no_fit: a candidate area exists but no matcher produced a hypothesis within
bounds. unverified: a hypothesis exists and no second family was available (one sheet, no record, no structure to
match), delivered as a candidate for review. contradicted: a second family disagreed (address outside, neighbours
do not tile), delivered for review with both facts. perspective: the input is an oblique view, located not
georeferenced. schematic: the sheet is not metric, delivered as a warped display with its residual. capability_missing:
the knowledge names a method the library lacks and the build was not run or failed, with the primitive named, which
is the one reason the system itself must act on rather than report. budget: the cap was reached. Counting
abstentions by reason over a batch is how the next version is chosen: the dominant reason is the next primitive
to build or the next reference to add; it is never fixed by lowering a threshold.
