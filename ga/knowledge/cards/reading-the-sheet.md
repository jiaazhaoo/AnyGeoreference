method: what to read first and what a reading may not contain
---
Read before computing: road names, house numbers, place names, landmarks, the north arrow (up, or rotated by how
much), the scale (1:1250, 1/500), printed grid figures or coordinates, the site marking (red line, hatching). A reading
returns pixel boxes on the sheet and never coordinates on the Earth. Page-wide words (title block, notes) help
retrieval; only words on the sheet itself may anchor a placement. A reader that finds no location cue has answered:
nothing on this sheet places it.
