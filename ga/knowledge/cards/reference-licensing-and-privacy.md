Open sources (OSM under ODbL, national open data under open government licences, GeoNames, Wikidata, Natural Earth)
may be cached, rendered and shown in public outputs with attribution. Licensed sources (premium national mapping,
address registers, commercial imagery) may be used for matching and verification but their extracts never leave the
machine: no tiles, no vectors, no rendered crops in a repository, a demo page or a paper; evidence images for a
public page are drawn over open layers. Personal data (addresses, occupiers, applicant names on drawings) stays in
the reference store and the private delivery. Caches are keyed by the request (source, bounding box, query, version)
so a rerun is free and the same, and so that what was fetched can be listed and purged. A public example uses
public maps (out-of-copyright sheets, open map renders) and public reference data only; the method does not change.
