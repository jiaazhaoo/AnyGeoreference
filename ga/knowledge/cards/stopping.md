method: when to stop and what to deliver
---
Stop when a placement is accepted by the policy with its second family, when every tool that could add a new family
has run, or when the cost cap is reached. Deliver one of three things: an accepted placement with its probability,
evidence and cost; a candidate placement marked for review with what is missing; or an abstention with the reason
(no cues, no candidate area, no truth). A planner's value on graded drawings was knowing when to stop (4 steps on
sheets with nothing to place) rather than extra placements; it wasted steps where tool contracts were too coarse.
