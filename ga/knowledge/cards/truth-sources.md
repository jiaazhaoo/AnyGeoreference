Rank truth sources: control points the user supplies > an external authority (a client's graded polygon, a gazetteer
entry, a known landmark, an archive's georeference) > agreement across sheets in the batch (edge matching between
adjacent sheets, a road continuing across sheets, another edition of the same sheet) > self-consistency. A published
archive georeference is an authority with a known bias (it may keep the printed datum, it may be corner-only); say so
when using it. One sheet alone has no batch agreement: without an external source its placement is delivered as
unverified, never as accepted. Three similar sheets allow a pilot on one and a check on the other two, and give batch
agreement as a second family. Truth has a digest: it is bound to the exact image it was made for, and a truth
record whose artefact changed is stale, not truth. Truth from different sources that disagree is itself a finding
to deliver, ranked, with the disagreement; it is never averaged.
