Rank truth sources: control points the user supplies > an external authority (a client's graded polygon, a gazetteer
entry, a known landmark) > agreement across sheets in the batch (edge matching between adjacent sheets, a road
continuing across sheets, another edition of the same sheet) > self-consistency. A published archive georeference is
an authority with a known bias (it may keep the printed datum); say so when using it. One sheet alone has no batch
agreement: without an external source its placement is delivered as unverified, never as accepted. Three similar
sheets allow a pilot on one and a check on the other two, and give batch agreement as a second family.
