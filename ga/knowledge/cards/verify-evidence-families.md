Evidence counts by family, not by count: two readings of one sheet by two models are one family (both see the same
ink); a reading and an address-register lookup are two; a fit's residual and its inlier ratio are one family
(self-consistency) however many numbers they produce. A placement is accepted only when a second family agrees
with the matcher that made it: a structure overlay for a text fit, an address point inside for a learned match,
another edition or the neighbour sheet for a graticule fit, a second matcher of a different kind for anything.
Self-consistency (residual, score, inlier ratio) proves the points agree with each other, not with the Earth: the
one-line-off graticule read had a 40 m residual and was 9 km wrong; the learned matcher's score 50 was 736 m wrong.
Every rule in the policy carries its measured accepted-wrong rate on a frozen benchmark, and a change that raises
missed errors is refused by the gate. Independence is judged by what the check consumed: a verification that used
the same cues as the fit is not a second family, however different its arithmetic.
